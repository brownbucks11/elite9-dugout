FRIDAY NIGHT LIGHTS---BATMAN VS SUPERMAN----CHARACTER RINGS
September 11 in Statesville, NC
General Alert
Last Updated 06/30/2026 4:44 PM

Pre-payment confirms your team’s entry and guarantees a spot in the field; however, pre-payment is not required to participate. Teams that have prepaid will be listed as confirmed on the registration list.

We accept a minimum of six (6) teams and a maximum of eight (8) teams for our Friday Night Lights tournaments. Teams that submit pre-payment will be confirmed and placed into the field first. Any remaining spots will be filled on a first-come, first-served basis from the who is play list on the website
All teams need to make sure that you have an updated roster online prior to this weekend's tournament.  If you need assistance, please email or call the Tournament Director and they will be happy to help you complete this process.  All teams must have a current insurance policy naming Top Gun Sports as additionally insured on file with the Top Gun Office prior to the start of the event.
All teams should be at the facility at least 1 hour before the scheduled game time.
Coaches - Please have your teams prepared to start their games earlier than the scheduled game time.
