Davie Youth Complex Fall Tournament Discount Program

Register early and save on your fall tournament schedule!

Tournament Entry Discounts Applies to all regular weekend tournaments listed under Davie Youth Complex.

  • Enter 3–5 tournaments: Save $50 on each tournament entry.
  • Enter 6 or more tournaments: Save $75 on each tournament entry.

    • Organizations bringing multiple teams can save even more!


  • When 2 or more teams from the same organization register for the same tournament, each team will receive an additional $25 discount on that event.

    • To qualify for these discounts, your team(s) must appear on the "Who's Playing" list by July 25, 2026. An pay online 

    This promotion applies only to regular weekend tournaments.

    This offer is not valid for:

  • Free Paid Berth Events
  • Zone Qualifiers
  • One-Day Events

    All teams should be at the facility at least 1 hour before the scheduled game time.
