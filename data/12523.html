Kindergarten & Under $0.00

1st - 8th grades $10.00

9th Grade Up  $12.00

We accept Cash, Debit, Credit Cards & Apple Pay at the gates. You will be charged a convenience fee when using these methods of payment with the exception of cash.

Coaches - Please have your teams prepared to start their games earlier than the scheduled game time.
